# Sycophancy vs. Reflexive Contrarianism (Tuning AI Posture)

> [!TIP]
> **The Problem Ryan Identified on *Transmutations***:
> When AI labs try to cure models of **sycophancy** (*"You're totally right! Great idea!"*), they often overcorrect into **reflexive contrarianism**—models that nitpick, lecture, invent false-positive objections, or treat every user prompt like an adversarial debate.

---

## 1. The Three Postures (And Where Models Go Wrong)

| Posture | What It Sounds Like | Why It Breaks Collaboration |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Sycophantic Yes-Man** | *"What a brilliant insight! You are completely right to store 20GB of models on a 16GB RAM disk!"* | Agrees with bad assumptions; hides real risks behind flattery. |
| **2. Reflexive Contrarian / Lecturer** | *"Actually, that framing is problematic. Not everything needs to be automated, and you should reconsider your workflow before proceeding."* | Triggers demand avoidance (PDA); wastes time arguing over intent instead of helping. |
| **3. Calibrated Peer (Our Target)** | *"Proceeding with the build. One heads-up: `rootfs` is a 16GB RAM disk, so I mounted the model folder to `/mnt/cache` instead."* | **One-pass action** + **evidence-backed correction** only when a real boundary is hit. |

---

## 2. How This Vault Enforces the "Calibrated Peer" Posture

We fix this in two places at the same time (see [[50.03-2026-10-19-Tri-Agent-Hardening-Plan-and-Walkthrough]]):

### Layer A: The Written Rulebook ([[40.01-Agent-Policy]] / `WORKSTYLE.md`)
Copied automatically into 6 apps via [[40.02-Policy-Sync-Target]]:
- **No Unsolicited Lectures or Checkpoints**: When the goal is clear, finish the whole job in one pass. Don't stop every 2 minutes to ask *"Would you like me to continue?"*
- **The "One Question" Rule**: Only pause to ask a question when:
  1. Two interpretations lead to totally different results, OR
  2. An action is destructive/irreversible (`H06`), OR
  3. A privacy/credential boundary requires human say-so.
- **Scout Chime-Ins Without Pedantry**: In [[10.03-Codex-CLI-and-Spark]], the fast `Spark` model can challenge or spot a risk, retrieve evidence, and hand it to the primary agent—who explicitly accepts, corrects, or rejects the flag rather than lecturing the user.

### Layer B: The Model Weight Pack ([[20.02-LoRA-Weight-Adapter]])
When prompt rules aren't enough because a model's base weights are stubborn:
- We train custom LoRA adapters (`qwen3.5-9b-hermes-lora-v2` and `v3`) on `gpu-server` ([[30.01-Host-Topology-MOC]]).
- And in [[10.04-Hermes-Agent]], we keep both **aligned models** (`qwen3.5-9b-hermes-v2`) and **unfiltered/heretic models** (`The-Defiant-Fable-Uncensored-Heretic`) inside a **Mixture-of-Agents (`moa`) council** so we can compare an unfiltered take against a structured take in one turn.
