# Session Ingestor (Unifying 794 Chats Across 9 AI Apps)

## What It Is in Plain English

When you jump between [[10.03-Codex-CLI-and-Spark]], [[10.04-Hermes-Agent]], [[10.02-Antigravity-AGY]], and [[10.05-OpenCode-and-Maestri]], your good ideas get trapped inside 9 different app folders.

Our **Session Ingestor** (`~/.agents/scripts/unify_sessions.py`) reads every app's native chat log, strips out any API keys into [[40.05-TouchID-Redaction-Vault]], removes duplicates, and saves all **794 conversations** into one clean, searchable archive (`~/.agents/sessions/`, backed up hourly to `gpu-server`).

---

## 1. Where the 794 Saved Sessions Come From

1. **[[10.03-Codex-CLI-and-Spark]]**: `394` chats
2. **[[10.04-Hermes-Agent]]**: `173` JSON logs (+ `486` SQLite `state.db` chats)
3. **SpecStory**: `68` project histories
4. **[[10.05-OpenCode-and-Maestri]]**: `61` chats
5. **[[10.02-Antigravity-AGY]]**: `56` brain transcripts
6. **Cursor / Gemini CLI / Claude Code**: `42` combined chats

---

## 2. How Background Auto-Tagging Works (`tag_hermes_sessions.py`)

To make hundreds of past chats easy to browse without burning cloud tokens, `tag_hermes_sessions.py` summarizes and tags each chat locally:
1. Opens `state.db` in **read-only mode** and scrubs out secrets first.
2. Uses **Apple Intelligence (`FoundationModels` on the Mac's Neural Engine)** via `AppleTagger.swift` to generate a 1-sentence summary and 3–6 topic tags in ~2 seconds per chat—using **zero RAM from oMLX** (see [[40.06-ANE-Hybrid-Inference-and-Retrieval]]).
3. Links matching tags straight to notes in this `[[wikilink]]` vault!
