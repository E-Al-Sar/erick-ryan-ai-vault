# LoRA Weight Adapter (Custom Voice & Habit Packs)

## What It Is in Plain English

Instead of spending thousands of dollars retraining a 9-billion-parameter [[20.01-Base-Model]] from scratch, a **LoRA Weight Adapter** is a small, snap-on **"habit & voice pack"** trained on our `gpu-server` (RTX 4070 12 GB VRAM).

Think of the [[20.01-Base-Model]] (`Qwen3.5-9B`) as a smart actor, and the **LoRA Weight Adapter** as their character direction: it teaches the model to stop bad habits (like searching 6 times in a row when a file doesn't exist) while keeping all its general knowledge intact.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Quick Name Check** (see [[00.01-Terminology-Disambiguation-Matrix]]):
> When we say **"adapter"** while talking about GPU training or `v2`/`v3`, we mean this **LoRA Weight Adapter**—not a [[40.02-Policy-Sync-Target]] (`adapters.json`) or a [[40.04-Session-Ingestor]] (`unify_sessions.py`).
