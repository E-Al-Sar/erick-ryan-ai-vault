# Base Model (The Raw AI Brains)

## What It Is in Plain English

A **Base Model** is the raw AI brain file sitting on disk. On its own, it doesn't do anything until an [[20.03-Inference-Provider]] (**oMLX** or **Ollama**) loads it into RAM/VRAM—and we can optionally snap a [[20.02-LoRA-Weight-Adapter]] ("habit & voice pack") onto it to improve how it follows instructions.
