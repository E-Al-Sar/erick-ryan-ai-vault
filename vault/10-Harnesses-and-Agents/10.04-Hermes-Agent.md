# Hermes Agent — Local Models, MoA Councils & Kanban

## What Role It Plays

**Hermes Agent** (by Nous Research) is our local-first [[10.01-Agent-Harness]] running across `workstation-mac` and `gpu-server` ([[30.01-Host-Topology-MOC]]). Because it talks directly to our own local [[20.03-Inference-Provider]] servers (**oMLX** and **Ollama**), it runs day-to-day automation, Kanban task boards, and multi-model councils at zero cloud cost.

## Coolest Feature: Mixture-of-Agents (`moa`) Councils

Hermes lets us run a **multi-model council** (`/moa <prompt>` or `hermes chat --provider moa -m ux-council`) where several different models weigh in before a final synthesizer combines the best points:
- **`ux-council` Preset** (used to critique and simplify this vault for Ryan & Erick!):
  1. `Qwen3.5-9B-The-Defiant-Fable-Uncensored-Heretic` (Creative / unfiltered storyteller on **oMLX**)
  2. `S1-mini-MLX-bf16` (Fast analytical reasoner on **oMLX**)
  3. `hermes-eserv:v2` (Our custom [[20.02-LoRA-Weight-Adapter]] on `gpu-server`)
  4. `gemini-3.8-flash` (Cloud UX & product lens)
  - **Synthesizer**: `hermes-qwen35-uncensored:9b` on `gpu-server`
- Every council run saves a full JSONL transcript in `~/.hermes/moa-traces/` so we can inspect what each individual model said.
