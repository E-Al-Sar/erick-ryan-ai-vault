# Antigravity (AGY) — The Big-Picture Planner

## What Role It Plays

**Antigravity (AGY)** is Google DeepMind's agentic coding [[10.01-Agent-Harness]] running on `workstation-mac` ([[30.01-Host-Topology-MOC]]). In our multi-agent setup ([[00.02-Architecture-Overview-MOC]]), Antigravity is the **heavy-duty architect, deep researcher, and visual builder**.

## How It Connects to the Rest of the Vault

- **App Data Folder**: `~/.gemini/antigravity/`
- **Chat Transcripts**: `~/.gemini/antigravity/brain/<conversation-id>/`
  - Every conversation log (`transcript.jsonl`) is automatically collected and secret-scrubbed by [[40.04-Session-Ingestor]].
- **Shared House Rules**: `~/.gemini/config/rules/AGENTS.md` (kept in sync with [[40.01-Agent-Policy]] via [[40.02-Policy-Sync-Target]]).
- **Shared Skills**: `~/.gemini/config/skills/` (linked to our 31 playbooks in [[40.03-Agent-Skill]]).

## Why We Route Heavy Work Here (From Our Oct 19 Quota Audit)

In [[50.02-2026-10-19-Session-Review-and-Quota-Audit]], we checked usage across all our AI subscriptions:
- **Plenty of Breathing Room**: While [[10.03-Codex-CLI-and-Spark]] kept hitting 5-hour usage lockouts, Antigravity still had **77% of its Gemini pool**, **82% of its Claude/GPT pool**, and **54% of its Argon pool** untouched.
- **Our Rule of Thumb**: Whenever a task needs reading 20+ files, organizing notes in this vault, or building a visual app from scratch, we send it to **Antigravity** first so Codex stays free for surgical code reviews.
