# OpenCode & Maestri — Offline Coding & Spatial Canvas

## 1. OpenCode Desktop (`OpenCode.app`)

**OpenCode** is a clean desktop [[10.01-Agent-Harness]] wired straight to our local [[20.03-Inference-Provider]] (**oMLX** on `workstation-mac`). It works 100% offline using local Apple Silicon models:
- **Default Coding Brain**: `gpt-oss-20b-MXFP4-Q8` ([[20.01-Base-Model]])
- **Fast Helper / Title Brain**: `S1-mini-MLX-oQ5e` (runs at **182–300 tokens/sec**)
- **Alternate Local Brains**: `gemma-4-12b-coder-fable5` and `Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored`
- **Shared Rules**: Loads [[40.01-Agent-Policy]] via `~/.config/opencode/opencode.jsonc`.
- **Chat Archive**: 61 past sessions collected by [[40.04-Session-Ingestor]].

---

## 2. Maestri Spatial Canvas (`Maestri.app`)

**Maestri** is a visual workspace that lets us arrange multiple terminal and AI agent panes side-by-side on an infinite canvas (similar to a live digital workbench):
- **Agent Presets**: Can spawn panes running [[10.03-Codex-CLI-and-Spark]], **OpenCode** (local models), or a standard shell.
- **Shared Skills**: Automatically reads all 31 playbooks in [[40.03-Agent-Skill]] across `.claude/skills`, `.agents/skills`, `.hermes/skills`, and `.gemini/config/skills`.
