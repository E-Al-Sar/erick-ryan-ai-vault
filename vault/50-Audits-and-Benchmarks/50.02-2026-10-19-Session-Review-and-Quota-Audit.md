# Oct 19 Audit: The 88.8% Maintenance Sinkhole & Benchmark Receipts

> [!TIP]
> **What Happened on Oct 19, 2026**: Two [[10.03-Codex-CLI-and-Spark]] chats got locked out by OpenAI's 5-hour usage cap at the exact same moment while waiting on a `gpu-server` benchmark. That prompted a full audit of where our AI time and money were actually going.

---

## 1. The Big Wake-Up Call: The "88.8% Maintenance Sinkhole"

We counted every AI message across 4.5 days (Oct 15 – Oct 19):
- **[[10.02-Antigravity-AGY]]**: `2,083` responses
- **Local oMLX ([[20.03-Inference-Provider]])**: `2,024` local responses
- **[[10.03-Codex-CLI-and-Spark]]**: `1,463` tool/chat events
- **[[10.04-Hermes-Agent]]**: `2,428` calls

**The Shocking Stat**: **88.8% of our Antigravity usage** was being spent *tinkering with the AI harnesses themselves* rather than building actual projects—and meanwhile, Codex was hitting 100% of its 5-hour cap just sitting in 45-second `sleep` loops waiting for `gpu-server` to finish tests!

**How We Fixed It** (see [[00.02-Architecture-Overview-MOC]] and [[70.01-Intelligence-Fit-to-Form-MOC]]):
- Point **Antigravity** (which still had **77–82% unused quota**) at real project deliverables and big multi-file builds.
- Never let **Codex** babysit background server jobs.
