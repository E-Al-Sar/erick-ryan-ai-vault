# Speed Audit: Doubling Local Model Throughput on Workstation-Mac (oMLX)

> [!TIP]
> **What We Tested**: How fast our local [[20.01-Base-Model]] brains actually run on `workstation-mac` (M5 Pro, 24 GB RAM) inside **oMLX** ([[20.03-Inference-Provider]]), and what was secretly slowing them down.

---

## 1. Local Speed Scorecard (`workstation-mac`)

| Local Brain ([[20.01-Base-Model]]) | Chat Size | Speed (Tokens/Sec) | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`S1-mini-MLX-oQ5e`** | 500–5,500 tokens | **`182–300 tok/s`** | Blazing fast; ideal for auto-tagging & titles. |
| **`Qwen3.5-9B-MLX-4bit`** | 25k–40k tokens | **`43.7–54.0 tok/s`** | Best speed-to-smartness balance for long chats. |
| **`Qwen3.5-9B-oQ6e`** | ~55,000 tokens | **`27.0–31.9 tok/s`** | Higher 6-bit precision for heavy tool calling. |
| **`Qwen2.5-Coder-14B`** | 21,000 tokens | `5.9 tok/s` | Too heavy for 24 GB RAM; moved to `gpu-server`. |

---

## 2. Four Hidden Bottlenecks We Found & Fixed

1. **The Ghost Computer Timeout**:
   - `oMLX` had `"distributed_inference_enabled": true` turned on, so every 5 seconds it froze waiting for a second Mac that wasn't plugged in. Turning it `false` stopped the freezes immediately.
2. **Pinning 6 Models at Once (`19 GB` RAM Eaten)**:
   - Keeping 6 models locked in RAM left only `2.5 GB` for macOS. Unpinning the extras freed **10.3 GB of RAM** right away.
3. **Cache Freeze Between Turns (`516ms` → `5ms`)**:
   - Raising `hot_cache_max_size` to `"10GB"` and turning off `hot_cache_write_through` made turn cleanup **100x faster**.
4. **Giant Tool Outputs Eating the Context Window**:
   - Each terminal command was dumping `4,096` tokens into the chat, filling up the 55k limit in just 3 steps. Capping `max_tool_result_tokens` at `1,500` stretched tool sessions from **3 turns to 8 turns** while offloading 32 layers of prompt reading to the **Apple Neural Engine (ANE)**.
